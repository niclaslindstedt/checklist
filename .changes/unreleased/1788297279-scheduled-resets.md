title: Scheduled resets
doc: scheduled-resets
---

Swipe a list right in the sidebar (or right-click it) and tap the clock to make it uncheck itself on a schedule — every so many days, weeks or months, or on chosen weekdays, at a time of day you pick — optionally popping the fresh list up the next time you open the app; the template button now lives on that same right swipe.
